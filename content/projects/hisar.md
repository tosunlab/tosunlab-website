End-to-end encrypted password, SSH-key and secrets manager for developers.

- **For agents:** `hisar mcp` gives coding agents your secrets without showing them.
- **For the shell:** `hisar run` swaps secret references in `.env` for real values.
- **Your server or ours:** one binary and Postgres. It only ever sees ciphertext.
- **Runs on:** macOS, Linux, iPhone, Android, Chromium, and Windows via the CLI.
